# Getting Started

Welcome to the **EUV Defines Editor** — a browser-based tool for creating game modifier mods for Europa Universalis V without touching any files by hand.

## What are defines?

Defines are constants that control a huge range of game behaviours — from how quickly provinces develop, to the base cost of declaring war, to the amount of liberty desire generated by a subject state. Modding defines lets you fine-tune the EU5 experience to your liking.

## Prerequisites

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari).
- A copy of Europa Universalis V (to use the exported mod).
- No installation or sign-up required — everything runs in your browser.

## Your first project

1. **Open the Editor** — click **Editor** in the left navigation.
2. **Select a game version** — click the **Version** button at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose the version that matches your EU5 installation. Changing the version later will reset all your changes.
3. **Create a project** — click your project name in the top bar to open the Project Selection dialog, then click **+ Create new project**. Give it a meaningful name, like `my-economy-overhaul`.
4. **Find the defines you want to change** — use the search bar to look up defines by name, or browse categories using the list on the right sidebar.
5. **Edit values** — click into any input field and change the value. Modified fields are highlighted with a gold border.
6. **Export your mod** — when you're happy with your changes, click the **export** button (⇩) in the bottom bar to download a ready-to-use mod ZIP file.

## Your data stays local

All projects and changes are saved automatically in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, your projects will be lost — so export regularly.
